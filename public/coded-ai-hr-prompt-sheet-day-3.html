How to use this
  1. Watch the trainer first. Then find the same card here.
  2. Download the file on the card. In Copilot Chat, start a new chat and attach it (the + or paperclip button).
  3. Click COPY, paste the request into Copilot, then press Enter.
  4. Check the answer against the file before you trust it: dates, numbers, names and rules.

Answers vary. Copilot may word things differently from the trainer's screen. That is normal, and it is why we check. Requests you write yourself are not on this sheet: write them with the checklist, and use the hints on the day's lab page if you are stuck. Use only the fictional Mutla files here, or green tasks from your own work.

Instructions
You are Mutla HR Policy Helper, an assistant for Mutla Oil Company employees. Employees ask about leave, allowances, attendance and training. Authorized HR staff decide every case. Answer each question from the knowledge files, with the policy name and section. Use only the five knowledge files. Allowances: version 3 applies until 31 December 2026; version 4 applies from 1 January 2027. Give today's rule first, then the change and its date. Never give or guess the salary, allowance or personal data of a named person. Never decide a case. If the answer depends on the person's grade or situation, ask first. If the files do not answer it, say so and refer the person to the right HR unit. Polite, short, plain English. Two to four sentences, then the source: policy name and section.
Starter prompt 1
How many days of leave can I carry over?
Starter prompt 2
When does allowances version 4 start?
Starter prompt 3
How do I ask for a training course?
What to look forWatch where each checklist part goes in the instructions.

Task 3.2Word, then Agent BuilderYour turn · 20 min

Write and build your agent

  1. In a blank document, write the instructions for Mutla HR Policy Helper with the five checklist parts: which files it uses, the dates of the two allowances versions, what it never answers, when it asks first, and where it refers people.
  2. In Microsoft 365 Copilot, choose Create agent, then the Configure tab. Add a name, a one-line description and your instructions.
  3. Upload the five policy files under Knowledge. Add three starter prompts.
  4. Ask one normal question in the test pane. Does the answer give the section?

Block 4 · Map a workflow

Demo 3.6Copilot ChatWatch, then try

A story into a step table

Watch it in the slide. The trainer runs it with the room.
Map a story
Turn the process story below into a step table. The story: To book an external trainer, the Training Coordinator emails three providers for prices. She copies each price into a spreadsheet by hand. Her manager approves the choice by email, usually after three days. She then emails Procurement, who asks for the same three prices again. Nobody tells the trainer the room number until the day before. Do not improve the process. Only map it. Flag each step that has a delay, duplicated effort, unclear ownership or a repetitive step. Mark every step where a person must decide. A table: step, who, what happens, input, output, wait, problem, a person decides?
What to look forA step nobody owns is still a step.
FilesNo file needed.
TakeawayMap it as it is. Flag the problems. Do not fix them yet.
