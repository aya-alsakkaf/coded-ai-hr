Your turn

4 hands-on tasks. The timer runs on the main screen. Every prompt is on your prompt sheet.

Your turn · Task 3.1 · 10 min · on your own

Save your best three

  1. Open your Prompt Bank (or mutla-catchup-prompt-bank.docx if you missed a day).
  2. Pick your three best tested requests.
  3. Save each one in the Prompt Gallery under Your Prompts, with a clear name.
  4. Organize the Prompt Bank by task: Draft, Compare, Summarize, Review.
10:00 on the clock · Copilot Chat · Prompt Gallery
You hand in: Three tested requests saved under Your Prompts and a Prompt Bank organized by task.
Look for: Name each prompt by its task, so you find it in a month.
Finished early? Share one prompt with a team, if your account has one.
mutla-prompt-bank-template.docxmutla-catchup-prompt-bank.docx
Your turn · Task 3.2 · 30 min · on your own

Build the Mutla HR Policy Helper

  1. New agent → Configure. Name it and write a one-line description.
  2. Paste the instructions from Demo 3.2 on the prompt sheet. Add the five policy files under Knowledge.
  3. In Try it, ask the three test questions. Write a test log: question, answer, pass or fix.
  4. Fix one instruction that failed, then test again. Create the agent.
30:00 on the clock · Agent Builder
You hand in: A working assistant and a three-line test log.
Look for: If it answers the salary question, add a clearer line under Must not answer.
Finished early? Extend the assistant to a second scenario: new joiners in their first month, with questions about leave, allowances and their first training course. Add two starter prompts for it, then run a fourth test with a question the files do not cover. It must refer the person to HR.

Your turn · Task 3.3 · 100 min · on your own

A tested workflow for a new case, in two parts

  1. Open the capstone case for your track. It is new to you. Part 1 runs to 11:35; part 2 runs 12:15 to 13:30.
  2. Plan the steps: which outputs, which app, which files.
  3. Write five-input requests for each step. Chain at least two Copilot apps.
  4. Review every output with the four-point check.
  5. Save the requests as a reusable prompt set in Word.
100:00 on the clock · Copilot Chat + the apps
You hand in: The outputs for your case and a reusable prompt set in Word.
Look for: Start with the plan request. It turns the case into steps.
Finished early? Connect the workflow to your HR assistant, or turn one request into a starter prompt.

Your turn · Task 3.4 · 20 min · in pairs

Peer test

  1. Swap with a colleague from a different unit.
  2. Run their prompt set on their track's fresh input in mutla-capstone-fresh-input.docx. Ask them nothing.
  3. Write down where it failed or where you had to guess.
  4. Swap back. Fix your set and write the fix into it.
20:00 on the clock · A colleague + Copilot
You hand in: One fix written into your prompt set.
Look for: Watch where your colleague stops to ask. That is your fix.
Finished early? Run a second colleague's set.

What you will be able to do

  • Find, adapt and save prompts in the Prompt Gallery.
  • Build an HR assistant and test it with a normal, an incomplete and a sensitive question.
  • Complete a new case by chaining two Copilot apps and reviewing every output.
  • Hand your prompt set to a colleague who can run it with no instructions.
