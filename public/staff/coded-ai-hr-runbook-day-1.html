02Demo 1.1 · Copilot ChatIn the slide

The same question, with and without the policy

Files: mutla-leave-policy-2026.docx
▶Say“Without the file Copilot predicts a likely answer. With the file and an instruction to cite it, you can check it in ten seconds.”
  1. Open a new Copilot Chat. Ask the question below with no file. Note the answer.
  2. Open another new chat. Attach mutla-leave-policy-2026.docx and paste the full request.
Prompts: paste in this order
No fileHow many days of unused annual leave can staff carry over to next year?
With the policyHow many days of unused annual leave can staff carry over to next year? Answer only from the attached mutla-leave-policy-2026.docx. Give the section number. If the policy does not say, write: not in the policy.
What you should seeWith no file, Copilot guesses a common rule, often 30 days or "it depends on your company". With the policy attached, it says 15 days, used by 31 March, and names section 4.

03Task 1.1 · Your eyes + the policyYour turn · 12 min · in pairs

Spot the confident mistakes

Files: mutla-leave-policy-2026.docx, mutla-copilot-leave-answer.docx
▶Say“The answer reads well. Three of its numbers are wrong. Fluent is not the same as correct.”
  1. Open mutla-copilot-leave-answer.docx. It is a Copilot answer to an employee's leave questions.
  2. Open mutla-leave-policy-2026.docx next to it.
  3. Check every number and rule in the answer against the policy. No AI for this step.
  4. Write each mistake: what the answer says, and the correct section.
Prompts: paste in this order
Then ask Copilot (only after you check by hand)Compare the attached answer with the attached leave policy. List every statement in the answer that does not match the policy, and quote the section that is right.
What you should seeThree mistakes: carry-over: the answer says “Staff can carry over up to 30 days of unused leave.” but Section 4: up to 15 days, used by 31 March; emergency leave: the answer says “Staff can take up to 5 days of emergency leave a year.” but Section 5: up to 3 days a year; notice for annual leave: the answer says “Staff should request annual leave at least 7 days before.” but Section 3: at least 14 days before.
+Finished earlyFor each mistake, write the one-line instruction that would have stopped it.
!If it failsIf a pair cannot open the files, use the printed copies from the facilitator kit. The result is on the backup results page.
mutla-leave-policy-2026.docxmutla-copilot-leave-answer.docx

06Task 1.2 · Your eyes + WordYour turn · 12 min · on your own

Redact a case note

Files: mutla-case-note-unredacted.docx
▶Say“Redact first, then ask. Copilot can help you tidy the note only after nothing sensitive is left in it.”
  1. Open mutla-case-note-unredacted.docx in Word. Save a copy with -redacted at the end of the name.
  2. Find every detail that identifies a person or is sensitive.
  3. Replace names with roles (Employee A, Colleague B). Remove the civil ID, phone number, salary and medical reason.
  4. Read it once more. Could anyone outside HR tell who this is?
Prompts: paste in this order
Only on the redacted copyRead the attached note. List any detail that could still identify a person or is sensitive. Do not repeat the detail itself; give its paragraph number.
What you should seeSix items removed or replaced: employee's full name → Employee A; colleague's full name → Colleague B; civil id number → [civil ID removed]; mobile number → [phone removed]; salary figure → [salary removed]; medical reason → [medical reason removed].
+Finished earlyOn the redacted copy only, ask Copilot whether any personal detail is left.
!If it failsIf Word is not available, mark the items on the printed note. The result is on the backup results page.
mutla-case-note-unredacted.docx
◆Land thisReplace names with roles. Remove IDs, numbers, salaries and medical details.
