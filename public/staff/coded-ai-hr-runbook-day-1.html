Pre-flight (30 minutes before)

  • Sign in to Microsoft 365 Copilot on the presenting laptop with the training account. Test one file upload (the leave policy). Every demo runs live.
  • Read the license label under your name on the Microsoft 365 home page: Copilot Chat (Basic), M365 Copilot (Basic) or M365 Copilot (Premium). Day 2 app demos need an M365 Copilot label (Basic or Premium); Teams recaps need Premium.
  • Day 3: open Agent Builder (Create agent) once before the day starts. If it is missing, use the fallback in the runbook.
  • Day 3: check that the Power Automate demo video plays with sound on the presenting laptop. Until the video is recorded, present the six steps on the slide.
  • Open this runbook, the backup results and the answer key on your own screen.
  • Copy today's sample files to the presenting laptop and to a USB stick.
  • Test the projector with the deck at full screen (F). Open the backup results for today on your own screen, in case Copilot is slow.

07Demo 1.3 · Copilot ChatIn the slide

Fix it with follow-up instructions

Files: mutla-role-information-2026.docx, mutla-jd-template-2026.docx
▶Say“The first draft is a draft. Say exactly what to change, and say what to keep. You do not start again.”
  1. Trainer: Open a new Copilot Chat. Attach mutla-role-information-2026.docx and mutla-jd-template-2026.docx. Paste the checklist request for the Compensation Analyst.
  2. Trainer: Read the draft aloud. Name one thing that is weak.
  3. Trainer: Send the follow-up that fixes it. Say which part of the draft you want kept.
  4. Trainer: Send the third follow-up: the list of assumptions. Delete what has no source.
Prompts: paste in this order
The checklist requestYou are an HR officer at Mutla Oil Company. It is for the 2026 job evaluation round. The Job Evaluation committee will read it. Draft the job description for the Compensation Analyst. Use the attached role information for the Compensation Analyst and the attached 2026 template. Use only the attached files. Do not add duties, a team size or a grade. Leave the grade line blank. One page. Formal, neutral, plain English. Each duty starts with a verb. The template's headings, in order. Start with the line: DRAFT · Reviewed by: ___
Follow-up · Fix what is missingThe role information has six duties. Your draft has five. Add the missing duty, word for word from the role information. Change nothing else.
Follow-up · Fix the shapeShorten it to one page. Keep every duty. Remove the introduction paragraph.
Follow-up · Show your guessesList every assumption you made that is not in the attached files. One line each.
What you should seeAfter the follow-ups: a one-page job description with the six duties from the role sheet, no introduction, the grade blank. The assumption list shows anything Copilot added on its own, for example a system name or years of experience.
!If it failsShow the backup list on the trainer screen. The result is on the backup results page.
