Pre-flight (30 minutes before)

  • Sign in to Microsoft 365 Copilot on the presenting laptop with the training account. Test one file upload (the leave policy). Every demo runs live.
  • Read the license label under your name on the Microsoft 365 home page: Copilot Chat (Basic), M365 Copilot (Basic) or M365 Copilot (Premium). Day 2 app demos need an M365 Copilot label (Basic or Premium); Teams recaps need Premium.
  • Day 3: open Agent Builder (Create agent) once before the day starts. If it is missing, use the fallback in the runbook.
  • Day 3: check that the Power Automate demo video plays with sound on the presenting laptop. Until the video is recorded, present the six steps on the slide.
  • Open this runbook, the backup results and the answer key on your own screen.
  • Copy today's sample files to the presenting laptop and to a USB stick.
  • Test the projector with the deck at full screen (F). Open the backup results for today on your own screen, in case Copilot is slow.

03Task 1.1 · Your eyes + the policyYour turn · 10 min · in pairs

Spot the confident mistakes

Files: mutla-leave-policy-2026.docx, mutla-copilot-leave-answer.docx
▶Say“The answer reads well. Three of its numbers are wrong. Fluent is not the same as correct.”
  1. Open mutla-copilot-leave-answer.docx. It is a Copilot answer to an employee's leave questions.
  2. Open mutla-leave-policy-2026.docx next to it.
  3. Check every number and rule in the answer against the policy, by hand. No AI for this step.
  4. Write each mistake: what the answer says, and the section that is right.
Prompts: paste in this order
Then ask Copilot (only after you check by hand)Compare the attached answer with the attached leave policy. List every statement in the answer that does not match the policy, and quote the section that is right.
What you should seeThree mistakes: carry-over: the answer says “Staff can carry over up to 30 days of unused leave.” but Section 4: up to 15 days, used by 31 March; emergency leave: the answer says “Staff can take up to 5 days of emergency leave a year.” but Section 5: up to 3 days a year; notice for annual leave: the answer says “Staff should request annual leave at least 7 days before.” but Section 3: at least 14 days before.
+Finished earlyFor each mistake, write the one-line constraint that would have stopped it.
!If it failsIf a pair cannot open the files, they read them on the Day 1 lab page. The result is on the backup results page.
