  9. Anything else L&D should know? (optional, no names)
Block 4 · Forms: training needs · Task 2.8

Build your form

Microsoft Forms · Copilot · input: mutla-training-needs-brief-2027.docx

No Draft with Copilot?

  1. Ask Copilot Chat: write my questions as a numbered list, each choice on its own line.
  2. Paste the list into Word and save it.
  3. In Forms, select Quick import and upload the file. Check every question it made.
  4. Number questions arrive as text: set them to Number by hand.

Block 5 · PowerPoint · Task 2.11

Fix a messy deck, or build your own

PowerPoint · Copilot Chat · input: mutla-q3-hr-update-messy.pptx, mutla-case-je-reevaluation-request.docx, mutla-case-compensation-allowance-update.docx, mutla-case-manpower-new-section.docx, mutla-case-personnel-attendance-change.docx, mutla-case-ld-training-requests.docx

A · What to fix in the messy deck

SlideProblemFix
1No meeting name or date. "Draft v2 FINAL (2)" in the subtitle.Add the meeting and the date. Remove the version note.
2One long paragraph that nobody can read from the back of the room.Four or five agenda points, a few words each.
3 and 4The same topic twice, with two numbers: 412 and "about 400". One filler sentence.One slide. The checked figure: 398. Cut the filler.
5Employee names and salaries in a table, in 8-point text.No names or salaries. Three payroll items with Compensation and Personnel to confirm.
6"Late arrivals down 45%" and "the pilot proves the rules work for everyone".38% in an 8-week pilot in the Finance division, as in the brief. One division only.
7Four fonts and four colors. "We hired a lot of people" with no number.One font, one color. A number from the source, or [number to confirm].
8Next steps with no owner and no date.An owner and a date for each step, or [owner to confirm]. Copilot must not invent them.
AllNo speaker notes on any slide.Two sentences of notes on every slide.

B · The open question in each case

Job Evaluation and Salary Scaling: The employee asks whether the grade will change. Only the committee decides.

Compensation: Employees will ask whether the change is automatic. It is: no request is needed.

Manpower: Two technician positions: contract or permanent? Not decided.

Personnel and Administration: How to handle exceptions to the new rules.

Learning and Development: Which requests are still pending, with KWD 3,100 left in the Q4 budget.
