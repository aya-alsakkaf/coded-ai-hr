Capstone · Task 3.3

A tested workflow for a new case, in two parts

Copilot Chat + the apps · input: mutla-capstone-je-salary.docx, mutla-capstone-compensation.docx, mutla-capstone-manpower.docx, mutla-capstone-personnel.docx, mutla-capstone-ld.docx, mutla-jd-template-2026.docx, mutla-job-evaluation-factors.docx, mutla-leave-policy-2026.docx, mutla-allowances-policy-v4.docx, mutla-training-policy-2026.docx, mutla-prompt-bank-template.docx

The five capstone cases

Job Evaluation and Salary Scaling: Three job descriptions for the new Finance structure. Finance has a new structure. Three jobs need updated job descriptions in the 2026 template, with evidence notes for the Job Evaluation committee. This happens every time a structure changes.

Compensation: Answering allowance questions every month. Every month, about 30 employees email Compensation with allowance questions. Build a reusable set that drafts replies from the allowances policy and a monthly summary for your supervisor.

Manpower: The monthly vacancy report for Production. Every month, Manpower sends the Production division a vacancy report: open positions, how long each has been open, and what is blocked. Build a reusable set for it.

Personnel and Administration: Monthly leave-balance reminders and exceptions. Every month, Personnel reminds employees with high leave balances to plan their leave, and lists exceptions for line managers. Build a reusable set for it.

Learning and Development: Course feedback summaries and nominations. After every course, L&D summarizes the feedback for the trainer and the unit heads, then sends nominations for the next course. Build a reusable set for it.
