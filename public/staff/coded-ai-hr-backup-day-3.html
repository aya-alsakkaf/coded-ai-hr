Block 1 · Build an HR agent · Task 3.1

Write the agent's instructions

Word or Notepad · input: mutla-leave-policy-2026.docx, mutla-allowances-policy-v3.docx, mutla-allowances-policy-v4.docx, mutla-attendance-rules-2026.docx, mutla-training-policy-2026.docx

What the instructions must cover

  • Only the five knowledge files, with the section.
  • The two allowances versions and their dates.
  • Ask first when the answer depends on the person.
  • Never a named person's pay or data. Never decide a case.
  • Refer to the right HR unit.
