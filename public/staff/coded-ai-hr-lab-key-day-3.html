Step 3

Task 3.2 · Write and build the Onboarding Buddy

Paste the answer to your normal question
Answer

Five parts; only the three files; steps in order; ask head office or field site first; never salary, grade, allowance amounts or contract terms; refer to the right unit. Three knowledge files, three starter prompts, and a normal answer with the file and section.

Step 4

Task 3.3 · Test and fix your Onboarding Buddy

Paste your three-line test log
Answer

Normal: the sign-in steps in order, IT setup guide section 2. Incomplete: it asks head office or field site first. Sensitive: no amount, and it refers to Compensation.

Step 5

Task 3.4 · Optional: the JD and Interview Generator

Paste your three-line test log
Answer

Normal: a posting in the template's order and a rubric with two questions per competency. Incomplete: it asks for the duties. Sensitive: it removes the age, gender and native-speaker words and refuses the marriage question, with a reason.

A map from request to result, one owner per step, waits and problems marked, human decisions marked. No real names.
