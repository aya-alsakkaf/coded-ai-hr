Task 1.3

Your job description, fixed and checked, with your request and your two follow-ups.
Task 1.3 · 22 min · on your own

Your job description: draft, fix, check

mutla-role-information-2026.docx, mutla-jd-template-2026.docx, mutla-jd-hr-officer-2019.docx
Hint (the participant pages show this)Data names both files. Constraints say what Copilot must not add: duties, a team size, a grade.
Answer

What a good request has

  • Role: an HR officer at Mutla.
  • Context: the 2026 evaluation round; the committee reads it.
  • Task: the job description for the track's role.
  • Data: both files attached and named.
  • Constraints: only the files; no duties, team size or grade added; one page.
  • Tone: formal, neutral, plain English.
  • Output format: the template's headings; starts with DRAFT · Reviewed by: ___

What a good result has

  • The template's eight headings, in order.
  • Only the duties in the role sheet (6 for every track's role), each starting with a verb.
  • No team size, no grade, no salary.
  • A neutral tone: no "dynamic", "exciting" or "world-class".

Answer

The planted problem

The policy title says version 4 applies from 1 January 2027. A summary that says "the housing maximum is KWD 300" with no date is wrong today: version 3 (KWD 250) applies until then. Accept a summary that gives both, with the date.

Numbers to check

  • Housing: 25% of basic salary, up to KWD 300 (section 2).
  • Transport: KWD 60 for grades 1 to 8 (section 3).
  • Remote site: 20% for more than 15 days in a month (section 5).
