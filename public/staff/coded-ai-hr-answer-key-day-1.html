One job description, three ways

Hint (the participant pages show this)Request 3 needs both files attached and all five inputs.
Answer

What a good Request 3 result has

  • The template's eight headings, in order.
  • Only the duties in the role sheet (6 for every track's role), each starting with a verb.
  • No team size, no grade, no salary.
  • A neutral tone: no "dynamic", "exciting" or "world-class".
