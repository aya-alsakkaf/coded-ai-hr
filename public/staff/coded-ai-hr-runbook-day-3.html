08Demo 3.5 · Power Automate · recorded demoIn the slide

A training request approval (recorded flow)

Files: power-automate-demo-1-training-request.mp4, mutla-training-request-log.xlsx
▶Say“A flow starts on its own and does the same steps every time. When a decision is needed, it stops and waits for a person.”
This demo must proveA flow starts on its own, does the same steps every time, and stops to wait when a person must decide.
Script (stage directions in brackets)
  1. (Play. Pause on the first card.) Four parts, the same in every flow: trigger, actions, connectors, output.
  2. (Pause on 1 · TRIGGER.) What starts it: a new answer in the training request form. Nobody presses a button.
  3. (Pause on Start and wait for an approval.) The flow stops here, for hours or days if needed, until a person decides.
  4. (Pause on the condition.) Approve takes one path. Reject takes the other.
  5. (Play to RESULT.) The approval took a minute and a half. Every other step took seconds. (Play to the end.) A row in Excel that nobody typed, and an email to the employee.
Discuss with the room
  • Which step did a person do? Listen for: The approval. Everything else is the same every time.
  • Where does a request like this wait in your unit today? Listen for: Signatures, email, printing and scanning.
Click steps
  1. Trainer: Click play on the video (no sound). Pause on each step card and name the part: trigger, action, connector, output.
  2. Trainer: Pause after Start and wait for an approval. Ask the room which step a person does.
  3. Trainer: Play to the end: the run took seconds except the approval. Point to the new Excel row and the email.
What you should seeThe steps: trigger: When a new response is submitted; action: Get response details; action: Start and wait for an approval; action: Add a row into a table; condition: Outcome is equal to Approve; action: Send an email (V2). One form response becomes one approval, one row in the log and one email. The approver decides; the flow only carries the request.
!If it failsIf the video does not play, open it from the Day 3 page (sample files) or the USB stick. If that fails too, walk through the four parts on the slide and the step table in the backup. The result is on the backup results page.
