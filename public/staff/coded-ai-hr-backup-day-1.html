Block 4 · Word, then Outlook · Demo 1.5

A policy summary with section numbers

Word · Copilot · input: mutla-leave-policy-2026.docx

Typical result

Section 2. Annual leave: Each employee has 30 days of annual leave a year.

Section 3. Requesting annual leave: Employees request annual leave in the HR system at least 14 days before the first day of leave. The line manager approves or declines the request.

Section 4. Carry-over: Up to 15 days of unused annual leave may be carried over to the next year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March. Days not used by then are lost.

Section 5. Emergency leave: Employees may take up to 3 days of emergency leave a year, with the line manager's approval on the same day.

Section 6. Sick leave: A medical certificate is needed for sick leave of more than 2 days in a row. Medical details stay with the Medical Services unit, not with the line manager.

Section 7. Hajj leave: Employees may take 21 days of paid Hajj leave once during their service.

Section 8. Records: Personnel and Administration records all leave in the HR system. Questions about a balance go to Personnel and Administration.

Block 4 · Word, then Outlook · Task 1.5

The allowances policy for staff

Word · Copilot · input: mutla-allowances-policy-v4.docx, mutla-allowances-policy-v3.docx

The facts the summary must match

Section 2. Housing allowance: Employees who do not live in Mutla housing receive a housing allowance of 25% of basic salary, up to a maximum of KWD 300 a month.

Section 3. Transport allowance: Employees in grades 1 to 8 receive a transport allowance of KWD 60 a month.

Section 4. Child allowance: Employees receive KWD 50 a month for each child under 18, for up to 3 children.

Section 5. Remote-site allowance: Employees assigned to a remote field site for more than 15 days in a month receive 20% of basic salary for that month.

Section 6. Requests and approval: Employees request allowances in the HR system. Compensation checks each request against this policy. The Compensation Supervisor approves.

Section 7. Changes in circumstances: Employees tell Personnel and Administration about any change that affects an allowance within 30 days.

Version 4 applies from 1 January 2027. Until then, version 3 applies.
