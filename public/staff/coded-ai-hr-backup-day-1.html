Block 4 · Word, then Outlook · Task 1.5

The training needs survey memo

Word · Copilot

A model request

You are the Head of Learning and Development at Mutla Oil Company. The memo goes to all line managers. Write a memo asking them to complete the 2027 training needs survey. The 2027 training needs survey opens on Monday 7 December 2026 and closes on Thursday 17 December 2026. It is in the HR portal, under Learning. Line managers add for each team member, one skill they need for their current job and one for the next two years, each marked needed now or later. L&D shares the 2027 training plan by Sunday 31 January 2027. Questions: Learning and Development, extension 4410. Use only these facts. Add no link, no name and no date. Keep every date as written. Formal and clear. A subject line, the steps as numbered points, under 200 words, starting with: DRAFT · Reviewed by: ___

Block 4 · Word, then Outlook · Demo 1.6

Summarize the leave policy for new joiners

Word · Copilot · input: mutla-leave-policy-2026.docx

Typical result

Section 2. Annual leave: Each employee has 30 days of annual leave a year.

Section 3. Requesting annual leave: Employees request annual leave in the HR system at least 14 days before the first day of leave. The line manager approves or declines the request.

Section 4. Carry-over: Up to 15 days of unused annual leave may be carried over to the next year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March. Days not used by then are lost.

Section 5. Emergency leave: Employees may take up to 3 days of emergency leave a year, with the line manager's approval on the same day.

Section 6. Sick leave: A medical certificate is needed for sick leave of more than 2 days in a row. Medical details stay with the Medical Services unit, not with the line manager.

Section 7. Hajj leave: Employees may take 21 days of paid Hajj leave once during their service.

Section 8. Records: Personnel and Administration records all leave in the HR system. Questions about a balance go to Personnel and Administration.
