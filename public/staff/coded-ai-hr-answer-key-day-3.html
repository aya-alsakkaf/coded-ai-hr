Task 3.1

The agent's instructions, in seven parts.
Task 3.1 · 12 min · on your own

Write the agent's instructions

mutla-leave-policy-2026.docx, mutla-allowances-policy-v3.docx, mutla-allowances-policy-v4.docx, mutla-attendance-rules-2026.docx, mutla-training-policy-2026.docx
Hint (the participant pages show this)Constraints hold the rules that protect people: never, ask first, refer.
Answer

A model set of instructions

PartInstructions
RoleYou are Mutla HR Policy Helper, an assistant for Mutla Oil Company employees.
ContextEmployees ask about leave, allowances, attendance and training. Authorized HR staff decide every case.
TaskAnswer each question from the knowledge files, with the policy name and section.
DataUse only the five knowledge files. Allowances: version 3 applies until 31 December 2026; version 4 applies from 1 January 2027. Give today's rule first, then the change and its date.
ConstraintsNever give or guess the salary, allowance or personal data of a named person. Never decide a case. If the answer depends on the person's grade or situation, ask first. If the files do not answer it, say so and refer the person to the right HR unit.
TonePolite, short, plain English.
Output formatTwo to four sentences, then the source: policy name and section.

Answer

A model set of instructions

PartInstructions
RoleYou are Mutla Job Description Coach. You help Mutla Oil Company HR staff and line managers draft job descriptions.
ContextThe drafts go to the Job Evaluation committee, which sets the level and the grade.
TaskDraft a job description from the duties the user gives you, in the 2026 template's order.
DataUse the 2026 template and the job evaluation factors file in your knowledge, and only the duties the user gives.
ConstraintsIf the user gives no duties, ask for them before you write. Add no duty, team size, grade or salary. If asked about a grade, say the Job Evaluation committee decides and refer the user to Job Evaluation and Salary Scaling.
ToneFormal, neutral, plain English.
Output formatThe template's headings in order, with the grade line blank. Start with: DRAFT · Reviewed by: ___

Task 3.6

A completed workflow card for your process.
Task 3.6 · 30 min · on your own

Final project, part 1: redesign your process

mutla-workflow-card-template.docx, mutla-process-stories.docx
Hint (the participant pages show this)Start with the steps you marked as a problem. Which one disappears, and which one becomes a flow?
Answer

A strong card has

  • A clear trigger and the inputs the request must contain.
  • Every step marked Person, Copilot, Agent or Flow, with one owner.
  • Every approval and decision with a person.
  • At least two problems from the map removed.
  • No real names or real data.

The example card

Card lineExample
Process and trackA training request · Learning and Development
Trigger: what starts itThe employee submits a training request form.
Inputs: what the request must containEmployee code, course name, course start date, reason, line manager's email. No names in free text.
Steps: each one marked Person, Copilot, Agent or Flow1 Form (Flow) · 2 Log the request (Flow) · 3 Line manager approves (Person) · 4 L&D approves (Person) · 5 Email the result (Flow, text drafted once with Copilot) · 6 Answer policy questions (Agent)
Decisions: which steps a person decidesThe line manager and L&D approve. The flow never approves.
Owners: one owner for every stepEmployee: the request. Line manager: step 3. L&D: step 4. Training Coordinator: the flow and the log.
Outputs: what the requester gets, and what is loggedThe employee gets an email with the decision. One row in the training request log.
Removed: the delays, duplicates and repeated steps you took outNo printing or scanning, no typing twice, a reminder after 2 working days instead of nobody chasing.
One piece you built: a Copilot request or agent instructionsAgent instructions for the Training Policy Helper: answers from the training policy, refers personal cases to L&D.
