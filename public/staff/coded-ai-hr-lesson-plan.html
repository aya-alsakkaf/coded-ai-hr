AI Fundamentals for HR · CODED · Tue 17 to Thu 19 November 2026 · 09:00-15:00 · CODED, Alsharekh room, Frontend Campus. CODED Professionals open workshop. 10 to 25 junior and middle-level HR employees, mixed AI familiarity, no coding. Story: Mutla Oil Company (fictional), five HR unit tracks. Tools: Microsoft 365 Copilot: Copilot Chat with file upload, Copilot in Word, Outlook, Excel and PowerPoint, built-in agents (Prompt Coach, Researcher) and Agent Builder. Copilot in Microsoft Forms, and Power Automate in recorded trainer demos. Habit: Copilot drafts. You check. Authorized HR staff decide.
Outcomes
  1. I can explain how Copilot produces an answer, what it can see, and why I check it.
  2. I can write a request with context, task, data, constraints and format, and fix a weak draft with follow-up instructions.
  3. I can use Copilot in Word and Outlook to draft HR documents and messages, and change the tone for each reader.
  4. I can keep personal, salary and case details out of prompts.
  5. I can use Copilot for meeting notes, follow-ups, Excel figures and PowerPoint summaries, and check every figure.
  6. I can map an HR process, build and test a simple HR agent, and tell when a Power Automate flow is needed.
