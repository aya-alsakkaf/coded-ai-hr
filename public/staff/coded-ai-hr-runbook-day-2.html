02Demo 2.1b · Copilot ChatIn the slide

Redact, then ask

No file needed.
▶Say“Copilot only ever sees the redacted note. The redaction happens before the prompt, not inside it.”
  1. Trainer: Show the note before redaction on the trainer screen only. Never paste it.
  2. Trainer: Redact it aloud: roles for names, no ID, phone, salary or medical detail.
  3. Trainer: Open a new Copilot Chat. Paste the request with the redacted note in it. Read the summary: does it guess the removed detail?
Prompts: paste in this order

Turn the note into a short, neutral case summary for the HR file: what happened, the request and the next step. Employee A, a technician, was absent on two days in late October. [Medical detail removed.] Employee A asks to work at a different site for one month. Colleague B can cover the shift. Use only the note. Do not guess any removed detail. Name nobody. Neutral, short, plain English. Three labeled lines, starting with: DRAFT · Reviewed by: ___
What you should seeA three-line summary (what happened, the request, the next step) that names nobody and does not guess the removed detail.

Redact a case note

Files: mutla-case-note-unredacted.docx
▶Say“Redact first, then ask. Copilot can help you tidy the note only after nothing sensitive is left in it.”
  1. Open mutla-case-note-unredacted.docx in Word. Save a copy with -redacted at the end of the name.
  2. Find every detail that identifies a person or is sensitive.
  3. Replace names with roles (Employee A, Colleague B). Remove the civil ID, phone number, salary and medical reason.
  4. Read it once more. Could anyone outside HR tell who this is?
Prompts: paste in this order
Only on the redacted copyRead the attached note. List any detail that could still identify a person or is sensitive. Do not repeat the detail itself; give its paragraph number.
What you should seeSix items removed or replaced: employee's full name → Employee A; colleague's full name → Colleague B; civil id number → [civil ID removed]; mobile number → [phone removed]; salary figure → [salary removed]; medical reason → [medical reason removed].
+Finished earlyOn the redacted copy only, ask Copilot whether any personal detail is left.

04Demo 2.2 · Copilot Chat · WordIn the slide

Agenda, brief and discussion points

No file needed.
▶Say“A meeting goes well when the page before it is good. Two minutes with Copilot, then you check the minutes add up.”
  1. Trainer: Open a new Copilot Chat (or a new Word document with Copilot).
  2. Trainer: Paste the request: the meeting facts are inside it.
  3. Trainer: Add up the minutes in the agenda aloud. Check that each open question became a discussion point.
Prompts: paste in this order
Prepare the meetingYou are a Personnel officer at Mutla Oil Company. We meet for 30 minutes on Monday to plan the orientation day for new joiners. Personnel, IT and L&D attend. Prepare an agenda, a one-paragraph background brief and three discussion points. The facts: 18 new joiners start on Sunday 6 December 2026. The orientation day runs from 8:00 to 13:00 in Training Room 2, head office. Personnel and Administration prepares the ID cards; IT prepares 18 laptop accounts. L&D runs the welcome session and the safety briefing. Open question: can the Medical Services check be done on the same day? Use only these facts. Give each agenda item an owner and minutes that add up to 30. Turn each open question into a discussion point. Short and practical. An agenda table (item, owner, minutes), then the brief, then the discussion points. Start with: DRAFT · Reviewed by: ___
What you should seeAn agenda with an owner for each item and 30 minutes in total, a short brief (18 new joiners on Sunday 6 December 2026, Training Room 2, head office), and three discussion points, one of them the Medical Services check.
!If it failsShow the backup agenda on the trainer screen. The result is on the backup results page.
◆Land thisAgenda, brief, discussion points: from approved facts only.
