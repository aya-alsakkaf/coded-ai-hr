01Demo 2.1 · Copilot Chat · WordIn the slide

Agenda, brief and discussion points

No file needed.
▶Say“A meeting goes well when the page before it is good. Two minutes with Copilot, then you check the minutes add up.”
This demo must proveCopilot prepares a meeting page in a minute, but only from the facts you give it. The trainer still checks the minutes, the owners and where the open question went.
Script (stage directions in brackets)
  1. (Show the request on screen.) Before a meeting I need three things: an agenda, a short brief and the questions we must discuss. The facts are inside my request, and nothing else.
  2. (Run it.) Under a minute. Now my part starts.
  3. (Add the minutes aloud.) Do they add up to 30? (Point to each owner.) Is every owner in the facts?
  4. (Point to the discussion points.) The Medical Services question is something to discuss. Copilot did not decide it for us.
Discuss with the room
  • Which of your regular meetings has no agenda today? Listen for: Weekly unit meetings, committee meetings, meetings called the same day.
  • What would you never put in a brief that goes to everyone at the meeting? Listen for: Names in a case, salaries, a decision that is not made yet.
Click steps
  1. Trainer: Open a new Copilot Chat (or a new Word document with Copilot).
  2. Trainer: Paste the request: the meeting facts are inside it.
  3. Trainer: Add up the minutes in the agenda aloud. Check that each open question became a discussion point.
Prompts: paste in this order
Prepare the meetingYou are a Personnel officer at Mutla Oil Company. We meet for 30 minutes on Monday to plan the orientation day for new joiners. Personnel, IT and L&D attend. Prepare an agenda, a one-paragraph background brief and three discussion points. The facts: 18 new joiners start on Sunday 6 December 2026. The orientation day runs from 8:00 to 13:00 in Training Room 2, head office. Personnel and Administration prepares the ID cards; IT prepares 18 laptop accounts. L&D runs the welcome session and the safety briefing. Open question: can the Medical Services check be done on the same day? Use only these facts. Give each agenda item an owner and minutes that add up to 30. Turn each open question into a discussion point. Short and practical. An agenda table (item, owner, minutes), then the brief, then the discussion points. Start with: DRAFT · Reviewed by: ___
What you should seeAn agenda with an owner for each item and 30 minutes in total, a short brief (18 new joiners on Sunday 6 December 2026, Training Room 2, head office), and three discussion points, one of them the Medical Services check.
!If it failsShow the backup agenda on the trainer screen. The result is on the backup results page.
◆Land thisAgenda, brief, discussion points: from approved facts only.

03Demo 2.2 · Copilot ChatIn the slide

Notes into decisions and actions

No file needed.
▶Say“A recap records what was agreed. An idea that nobody agreed to is not an action, however good it sounds.”
This demo must proveA recap holds only what people agreed. An idea nobody agreed is not an action, and a missing deadline stays missing.
Script (stage directions in brackets)
  1. (Paste the request with the notes.) These are rough notes, the kind we all write.
  2. (Run it. Read the action table aloud.) Four actions. One says: no deadline set. That is honest, and it is right.
  3. (Find the CEO line in the notes.) Somebody had an idea. Nobody agreed. It is not in the table, and if it were, I would delete it.
Discuss with the room
  • Who writes the recap in your team today, and how long after the meeting does it go out? Listen for: Often nobody, or days later. That is the gap Copilot closes.
  • What happens when a recap gives someone a task they never agreed to? Listen for: People argue about the recap instead of doing the work.
Click steps
  1. Trainer: Open a new Copilot Chat. Paste the request: the notes are inside it.
  2. Trainer: Read the action table aloud. Ask the room: is there an action nobody agreed?
Prompts: paste in this order
Notes into actionsTurn the meeting notes below into decisions, a table of action, owner and deadline, and the open questions. The notes: Fatma (Personnel): the ID cards will be ready by Thursday 3 December. IT (Hamad): 18 laptop accounts, ready by Wednesday 2 December. Noura (L&D): I will send the agenda to the new joiners by Monday 30 November. Someone said we could also invite the CEO. Nobody agreed to ask. Medical Services check on the same day: not decided. Fatma will ask Medical Services. Add no action that nobody agreed. If an action has no deadline, write: no deadline set. Decisions, then the action table, then the open questions.
What you should seeFour actions with owners; three have deadlines and one says no deadline set. The idea of inviting the CEO is not an action: nobody agreed. One open question: the Medical Services check.
!If it failsShow the backup table on the trainer screen. The result is on the backup results page.

11Demo 2.7 · Copilot ChatIn the slide

From a vague ask to requirements

Files: mutla-training-needs-brief-2027.docx
▶Say“A form is only as good as the decisions behind it. Write the decisions first, and the questions almost write themselves.”
This demo must proveA form is only as good as the decisions behind it. A vague request gives a generic survey; requirements first give questions that each serve a decision, with no personal data.
Script (stage directions in brackets)
  1. (Run the weak request, no file.) A training survey in ten seconds. Let us read it.
  2. (Point to each question.) Who answers this: staff or managers? Which decision does it help L&D make? (Point to any name or rating question.) Would you collect this?
  3. (New chat. Attach the brief. Run the requirements request.) Now Copilot starts from the decisions L&D must make.
  4. (Read the table row by row.) Every question has a decision next to it. If a row has no decision, we delete it.
  5. (Close.) This is the brainstorm. The form comes after it, not before.
Discuss with the room
  • Last time your team ran a survey, what did you do with the answers? Listen for: Often nothing, or the answers were too messy to count.
  • Should a training needs form ask for the employee's name? Listen for: No. Managers answer for the team. Names belong in nomination, later, through the approved process.
  • For your own unit, what is the one decision a training needs form must help you make? Listen for: Which course first, how many seats, when.
Click steps
  1. Trainer: Open a new Copilot Chat. Paste the weak request first, with no file. Read the questions aloud.
  2. Trainer: Ask the room: which of these questions helps L&D decide anything? Who would answer it, staff or managers?
  3. Trainer: Open a new chat. Attach mutla-training-needs-brief-2027.docx. Paste the requirements request. Read the table row by row.
  4. Trainer: Point to each question and ask: which decision does it serve? Delete any question with no decision behind it.
Prompts: paste in this order
A weak startCreate a training needs survey for our employees.
Requirements firstYou are a Learning and Development specialist at Mutla Oil Company. L&D builds the 2027 training plan from a training needs survey that 52 line managers answer in December. Last year's survey was a Word form, and the answers were hard to count. Before writing any question, list the decisions L&D must make from this survey and the information each decision needs. Then suggest one question for each piece of information. Use only the attached brief, mutla-training-needs-brief-2027.docx. At most 10 questions. Every question serves one decision. One idea per question. Use choices wherever the brief gives a list. Never ask for an employee's name, civil ID, performance rating, salary or health. A table: decision, information needed, question, question type (choice, number or text), required (yes or no).
What you should seeThe weak request gives a generic employee survey: name, job title, rate your skills, which courses you like. Nothing ties to a decision, and some questions collect personal data. The requirements request gives the five decisions in the brief and eight or nine questions: unit, section, team size, the skill needed now, how many need it, the skill for two years, how to learn, the best quarter, and an optional comment.
