Before Day 1

confirm by Sunday 15 November
  • The three portal surveys are linked to cohort AIFHR-Nov/Nov-26. The QR codes on the slides open them.
  • Send the joining note: bring a laptop signed in to Microsoft 365 with Copilot, charged, and know your password.
  • Ask participants to check the label under their name on the Microsoft 365 home page before Day 1: Copilot Chat (Basic), M365 Copilot (Basic) or M365 Copilot (Premium).
  • Keep 5 backup laptops with working accounts. Past Copilot rooms had uneven licenses and hit daily limits on Day 2.

Room and laptops

pre-flight
  1. Tables of four, by unit track where possible, facing the screen. Pairs sit side by side for Tasks 1.1, 2.1, 2.4 and 3.4.
  2. Wi-Fi name and password on the board before 08:45.
  3. Day 1, 09:00: every laptop opens Copilot Chat and is signed in. The pre-MAP QR at 09:10 is also the pre-flight.
  4. Print one safe-use card and one cheat sheet per person.

Plan A, B, C
What failsPlan BPlan C

Capstone

Day 3
  • Each participant takes the capstone file for their unit track. It is new to them.
  • Core: two Copilot apps, five-input requests, every output reviewed.
  • Extension: connect to the HR assistant, or a starter prompt.
  • Peer test at 13:30: pairs from different units. No scoring; the self-check is on the Day 3 lab page.
