Answer

What to check in each output

  • Every date and number matches the case file.
  • No named salary, no personal record, no medical detail.
  • No decision that belongs to a person (a grade, an exception, a promise).
  • Each output fits its reader: staff, a manager, Finance or a committee.

Job Evaluation and Salary Scaling · A re-evaluation request for the Senior Personnel Officer job

  • Personnel and Administration asks for a re-evaluation of the Senior Personnel Officer job.
  • Reason given: the job now handles the new attendance system and all employment letters.
  • The current job description dates from 2021.
  • The Job Evaluation committee meets on Thursday 10 December.
  • The employee asks if the grade will change. Only the committee decides.

Compensation · The housing allowance increase

  • From 1 January 2027, the housing allowance maximum rises from KWD 250 to KWD 300 a month.
  • 140 employees are paid at the current maximum today. All 140 have a basic salary of KWD 1,200 or more, so each gains the full KWD 50.
  • Extra cost: 140 × KWD 50 = KWD 7,000 a month, KWD 84,000 a year.
  • Finance asked for the cost estimate by Monday 30 November.
  • Employees will ask whether the change is automatic. It is: no request is needed.

Manpower · Staffing the new Drilling Support section

  • Drilling Operations will open a new Drilling Support section on 1 March 2027.
  • The approved structure has 12 positions: 1 section head, 3 engineers, 6 technicians, 2 coordinators.
  • Two technician positions may be contract, not permanent. Not decided.
  • Finance needs the manpower request by Thursday 26 November.
  • Recruitment wants the hiring order: section head first, then engineers.

Personnel and Administration · Rolling out the new attendance rules

  • The new attendance rules start on Tuesday 1 December 2026.
  • Flexible start between 6:30 and 8:00; core hours 8:00 to 13:00.
  • Up to 3 permissions a month, each up to 2 hours.
  • The staff announcement goes out by Sunday 22 November.
