Task 2.9

A: three lines with no employee codes. B: the memo rewritten, with the two differences flagged. C: five bullets with section numbers.
Task 2.9 · 10 min · on your own

Rewrite or summarize

mutla-overtime-memo-draft.docx, mutla-hr-quarterly-report-q3-2026.docx
Hint (the participant pages show this)A: who reads a team channel? B: the approved rules win. C: every bullet needs its section.
Answer

A · Check the update for

  • Three lines, every number as in the summary.
  • No employee codes and no names.
  • No blame on a unit or a person.

B · Two rules the memo gets wrong

RuleThe memoApproved
Daily limitThe memo says 5 hours a day.The approved rules say 4.
Claims deadlineThe memo says within 10 days.The approved rules say 5 working days after the month ends.
Accept a rewrite that uses the approved rules and says the memo differed, so the Superintendent can confirm. A rewrite that keeps 5 hours or 10 days has copied the error.

C · A good summary

  • Five bullets, each with its section number.
  • Every number as written in the report.
  • The open risks named (section 8), not only the good news.
  • No new facts and no opinions.
