Part 2 · Six HR requests that work
Copy, then change the details
Each one has the five inputs.
Job description
Role sheet + template
Draft a job description from the attached role information, in the template's order. Leave the grade blank.
Then trace every duty to the role sheet.
Compare versions
Two policy files
List every change between the two versions with its section number: section, old, new.
Open both files and check each change.
Warmer, same facts
Your announcement
Make it warmer; keep every date, time and rule as written.
The facts stay. Only the tone changes.
Meeting recap
A transcript or notes
Decisions, then a table of action, owner, deadline, then open questions. Add nothing nobody agreed.
Check each owner and date.
Tracker figure
An Excel file
Count by unit and show the rows you counted. Then compare with the control total.
A total that is too high: look for duplicates.
Investigation notes
Notes with roles, no names
Timeline and open questions. No finding, no blame, no recommended action.
People investigate and decide.
Copy the shape, not the words. Your unit and your files change the details.
Part 3 · The four-point review
Before anything goes out
Four checks, every output.
Accuracy
Every date, number, name and rule matches the source.
Confidentiality
No civil ID, named salary, medical detail or case detail that names a person.
Neutrality
No opinion, blame, promise or decision that belongs to a person.
Source alignment
Every point traces back to an approved file. No source, no line.
Copilot drafts. You check. Authorized HR staff decide.
