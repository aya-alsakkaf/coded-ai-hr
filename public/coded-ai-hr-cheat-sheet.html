Part 1 · Ask well
The prompt checklist
Five parts. Use all five for anything that matters.
Context
Who you are, who reads it, and why
You are an HR officer at Mutla Oil Company. It is for the 2026 job evaluation round, and the committee reads it.
Task
The one job to do
Update the job description for the Compensation Analyst.
Data
The facts and the files to use
Use the attached role information and the 2026 template.
Constraints
The limits it must keep
Use only the attached files. No team size, no grade. One page.
Format
How it looks and sounds
Formal and neutral. The template's headings in order. Start with: DRAFT · Reviewed by: ___
Context · Task · Data · Constraints · Format.
Part 2 · Six HR requests that work
Copy, then change the details
Each one has the parts that matter most for it.
Job description
Role sheet + template
Draft the job description from the attached role information, in the template's order. Use only the attached files. Leave the grade blank.
Then trace every duty to the role sheet.
Fix with a follow-up
The same chat
The draft is missing one duty from the role sheet. Add it, word for word. Change nothing else.
Name the problem, and say what to keep.
Warmer, same facts
Your announcement
Rewrite it for line managers. Keep every date, time and rule as written.
The facts stay. Only the tone changes.
Summary with sections
One policy
Summarize the attached policy for staff. Give the section number for every point. Keep every number as written.
Then check two numbers yourself.
Flag, do not fix
A request and its record
List every point where the request differs from the record or breaks the template rules. Do not change anything.
Copilot lists. A person decides.
Tracker figure
An Excel file
Count by unit and show the rows you counted. Then compare with the control total.
A total that is too high: look for duplicates.
Copy the shape, not the words. Your unit and your files change the details.
Part 3 · The four-point review
Before anything goes out
Four checks, every output.
Accuracy
Every date, number, name and rule matches the source.
Confidentiality
No civil ID, named salary, medical detail or case detail that names a person.
Neutrality
No opinion, blame, promise or decision that belongs to a person.
Source alignment
Every point traces back to an approved file. No source, no line.
Copilot drafts. You check. Authorized HR staff decide.
Part 4 · Where Copilot sits
Know your tools
Read the label under your name first.
Copilot Chat
Every license
Upload files, ask, draft. Your work files only when you upload them on a Basic label.
Copilot Chat (Basic), M365 Copilot (Basic) or M365 Copilot (Premium).
Built-in agents
Agents, then All agents
Agents Microsoft built for one job: Prompt Coach for better requests, Researcher for reports with sources.
Researcher and Analyst need the Microsoft 365 Copilot license.
Copilot in the apps
Word, Outlook, Excel, PowerPoint
Draft, summarize and analyze inside the app.
What you get depends on your license.
Agent Builder
Create agent, Configure, Test
An agent with your instructions and approved files that answers the same questions for many people.
It answers. It does not send, route or approve.
Power Automate
A flow, not a chat
Runs on its own when something happens: a form is sent, an email arrives, a date comes. Routes approvals, logs rows, sends emails, and can include an AI step.
Ask your IT team before you build one.
The same checklist works in every Copilot tool.
