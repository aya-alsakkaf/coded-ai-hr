Directing Copilot for Reliable HR Work

How Copilot writes, what it can see, what never goes in a prompt, and the five inputs that make an HR draft reliable.

Day 1 of 3 · Tuesday 17 Nov 09:00-15:00 In person · CODED, Alsharekh room, Frontend Campus
The day in one line · 09:00-15:00

Day brief

Day 1 starts with one job description and two requests. Then you see how Copilot writes and what it can see. You sort HR tasks before you type, learn the five inputs, and check a draft line by line against its source. You leave with a checked job description and the first entry in your HR Prompt Bank.

You build today: A checked job description and Prompt Bank entry 1.

Your turn

4 hands-on tasks. The timer runs on the main screen. Every prompt is on your prompt sheet.

Your turn · Task 1.1 · 12 min · in pairs

Spot the confident mistakes

  1. Open mutla-copilot-leave-answer.docx. It is a Copilot answer to an employee's leave questions.
  2. Open mutla-leave-policy-2026.docx next to it.
  3. Check every number and rule in the answer against the policy. No AI for this step.
  4. Write each mistake: what the answer says, and the correct section.
12:00 on the clock · Your eyes + the policy
You hand in: The three wrong statements, each with the correct section.
Look for: There are three mistakes: one about carry-over, one about emergency leave and one about notice.
Finished early? For each mistake, write the one-line instruction that would have stopped it.
mutla-leave-policy-2026.docxmutla-copilot-leave-answer.docx
Your turn · Task 1.2 · 12 min · on your own

Redact a case note

  1. Open mutla-case-note-unredacted.docx in Word. Save a copy with -redacted at the end of the name.
  2. Find every detail that identifies a person or is sensitive.
  3. Replace names with roles (Employee A, Colleague B). Remove the civil ID, phone number, salary and medical reason.
  4. Read it once more. Could anyone outside HR tell who this is?
12:00 on the clock · Your eyes + Word
You hand in: A redacted copy of the case note that is safe to use with Copilot.
Look for: Six items: two names, an ID number, a phone number, a salary and a medical reason.
Finished early? On the redacted copy only, ask Copilot whether any personal detail is left.
mutla-case-note-unredacted.docx
Your turn · Task 1.3 · 25 min · on your own

One job description, three ways

  1. Pick your unit track. Find your track's role in the role information file.
  2. Request 1: one line only, no files. Save the answer in Word.
  3. Request 2: a structured request with instructions, context and tone. No files.
  4. Request 3: all five inputs, with mutla-role-information-2026.docx and mutla-jd-template-2026.docx attached.
  5. Write one sentence: which version is best, and why.
25:00 on the clock · Copilot Chat
You hand in: Three versions of one job description, and one sentence on which is best and why.
Look for: Request 3 needs both files attached and all five inputs.
Finished early? Turn your best version into vacancy text for a job board, keeping every duty and adding no promises.
mutla-role-information-2026.docxmutla-jd-template-2026.docxmutla-jd-hr-officer-2019.docx
Your turn · Task 1.4 · 15 min · on your own

Check, fix and save

  1. Use your Request 3 job description from Task 1.3, or mutla-catchup-jd-draft.docx if you missed it.
  2. Run the source-trace request on it.
  3. Delete or fix every NOT IN SOURCE line. Check the grade is blank.
  4. Open mutla-prompt-bank-template.docx. Save your best request as entry 1: task, request, files, check.
15:00 on the clock · Copilot Chat + Word
You hand in: A corrected job description and Prompt Bank entry 1.
Look for: A team size and a grade that the role information does not give.
Finished early? Ask Copilot to list every assumption it made in your job description, then remove each one by adding the missing context.

What you will be able to do

  • Explain why Copilot can write a confident sentence that is wrong, and what it can see.
  • Sort HR tasks before you type, and redact a note before Copilot sees it.
  • Write a request with the five inputs.
  • Ask for the source of every line, and delete what has none.
