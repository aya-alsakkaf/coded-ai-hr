Copilot Foundations for HR Documents and Communication

How Copilot writes and what it can see, the prompt checklist, follow-up instructions, and HR documents and messages in Word and Outlook.

Day 1 of 3 · Tuesday 17 Nov 09:00-15:00 In person · CODED, Alsharekh room, Frontend Campus
The day in one line · 09:00-15:00

Day brief

Day 1 starts with one job description and two requests. You see how Copilot writes, what it can see, and which HR decisions it must never make. You learn the prompt checklist, fix weak drafts with follow-up instructions, and check every line against its source. Then you write a job description and an experience letter in Word, a policy summary for staff, and one announcement in Outlook for three different readers.

You build today: A checked job description, an experience letter, a policy summary, an announcement for three readers, and two Prompt Bank entries.

Your turn

6 hands-on tasks. The timer runs on the main screen. Every prompt is on your prompt sheet.

Your turn · Task 1.1 · 12 min · in pairs

Spot the confident mistakes

  1. Open mutla-copilot-leave-answer.docx. It is a Copilot answer to an employee's leave questions.
  2. Open mutla-leave-policy-2026.docx next to it.
  3. Check every number and rule in the answer against the policy, by hand. No AI for this step.
  4. Write each mistake: what the answer says, and the section that is right.
12:00 on the clock · Your eyes + the policy
You hand in: The three wrong statements, each with the section that is right.
Look for: There are three mistakes: one about carry-over, one about emergency leave and one about notice.
Finished early? For each mistake, write the one-line constraint that would have stopped it.

Your turn · Task 1.2 · 14 min · on your own, then swap

Rewrite weak requests

  1. Pick two weak requests: one close to your unit, and one more.
  2. Rewrite each with all seven parts. Name the file to attach.
  3. Swap with a neighbor. Then run one rewrite in Copilot Chat and check it.
14:00 on the clock · Copilot Chat
You hand in: Two rewrites with all seven parts.
Look for: For each weak request ask seven questions: who, why, what, with what, within what limits, in what voice, in what shape.
Finished early? Rewrite a third weak request, from a unit that is not yours.

Your turn · Task 1.3 · 22 min · on your own

Your job description: draft, fix, check

  1. Pick your unit track. Find your track's role in the role information file.
  2. Write your own checklist request (all seven parts). Attach the role information and the 2026 template.
  3. Read the draft. Send two follow-up instructions that fix what is weak.
  4. Run the source-trace request below. Delete or fix every NOT IN SOURCE line.
  5. Check by hand: count the duties against the role sheet. Is the grade blank? Does it start with DRAFT · Reviewed by: ___?
22:00 on the clock · Copilot Chat · Word
You hand in: Your job description, fixed and checked, with your request and your two follow-ups.
Look for: Data names both files. Constraints say what Copilot must not add: duties, a team size, a grade.
Finished early? Turn your job description into vacancy text for a job board. Keep every duty. Add no promise, salary or grade.

The allowances policy for staff

  1. Attach mutla-allowances-policy-v4.docx to a new chat, or open it in Word with Copilot.
  2. Write your own checklist request: a one-page summary of the policy for staff, every point with its section number.

Announce and reply in Outlook

What you will be able to do

  • Explain why Copilot can write a confident sentence that is wrong, what it can see, and which HR decisions stay with people.
  • Write a request with the prompt checklist: role, context, task, data, constraints, tone and output format.
  • Improve a weak draft with follow-up instructions, and ask for the source of every line.
  • Draft HR documents in Word and messages in Outlook, and change the tone for each reader without changing the facts.
