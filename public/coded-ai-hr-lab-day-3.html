Step 2

Task 3.2 · Write and build your agent

  1. About mutla-leave-policy-2026.docx: Mutla's approved leave policy: annual, emergency, sick and Hajj leave, and carry-over.
  2. About mutla-allowances-policy-v3.docx: The allowances policy in force today, version 3.
  3. About mutla-allowances-policy-v4.docx: The new allowances policy, version 4, approved this year.
  4. About mutla-attendance-rules-2026.docx: The approved attendance rules: start times, core hours and permissions.
  5. About mutla-training-policy-2026.docx: The training policy: how employees request and attend training.
  6. Write the instructions for Mutla HR Policy Helper with the five checklist parts: which files, the dates of the two allowances versions, what it never answers, when it asks first, and where it refers.
  7. In Microsoft 365 Copilot, choose Create agent, then the Configure tab. Add a name, a one-line description and your instructions. Upload the five policy files under Knowledge.
  8. Add three starter prompts. Ask one normal question in the test pane.

Data: version 3 applies until 31 December 2026; version 4 from 1 January 2027. Format: a short answer, then the policy name and section.

Still stuck? Show the prompt
You are Mutla HR Policy Helper, an assistant for Mutla Oil Company employees. Employees ask about leave, allowances, attendance and training. Authorized HR staff decide every case. Answer each question from the knowledge files, with the policy name and section. Use only the five knowledge files. Allowances: version 3 applies until 31 December 2026; version 4 applies from 1 January 2027. Give today's rule first, then the change and its date. Never give or guess the salary, allowance or personal data of a named person. Never decide a case. If the answer depends on the person's grade or situation, ask first. If the files do not answer it, say so and refer the person to the right HR unit. Polite, short, plain English. Two to four sentences, then the source: policy name and section.

Check my card
Check the attached workflow card. List any step with no owner, any decision that is given to Copilot or an agent instead of a person, and any problem from the map that is still in the process. Do not rewrite the card.
Stuck? Show a hint

Copilot: one-off drafting or summarizing. Agent: the same questions or the same kind of draft for many people. Person: approving, deciding, anything about one person's case.

Still stuck? Show another hint

Start with the steps you marked as a problem. A step that types the same data again can often disappear.
