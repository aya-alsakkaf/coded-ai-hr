Step 1

Task 3.1 · Write the agent's instructions

  1. Write the instructions for Mutla HR Policy Helper with the seven checklist parts.
  2. Say which files it may use, and the dates of the two allowances versions.
  3. Say what it must never answer, when it must ask first, and where it refers people.

Stuck? Show a hint

Constraints hold the rules that protect people: never, ask first, refer.

Still stuck? Show another hint

Data: version 3 applies until 31 December 2026; version 4 from 1 January 2027. Output format: a short answer, then the policy name and section.

Still stuck? Show the prompt
You are Mutla HR Policy Helper, an assistant for Mutla Oil Company employees. Employees ask about leave, allowances, attendance and training. Authorized HR staff decide every case. Answer each question from the knowledge files, with the policy name and section. Use only the five knowledge files. Allowances: version 3 applies until 31 December 2026; version 4 applies from 1 January 2027. Give today's rule first, then the change and its date. Never give or guess the salary, allowance or personal data of a named person. Never decide a case. If the answer depends on the person's grade or situation, ask first. If the files do not answer it, say so and refer the person to the right HR unit. Polite, short, plain English. Two to four sentences, then the source: policy name and section.

You are Mutla Job Description Coach. You help Mutla Oil Company HR staff and line managers draft job descriptions. The drafts go to the Job Evaluation committee, which sets the level and the grade. Draft a job description from the duties the user gives you, in the 2026 template's order. Use the 2026 template and the job evaluation factors file in your knowledge, and only the duties the user gives. If the user gives no duties, ask for them before you write. Add no duty, team size, grade or salary. If asked about a grade, say the Job Evaluation committee decides and refer the user to Job Evaluation and Salary Scaling. Formal, neutral, plain English. The template's headings in order, with the grade line blank. Start with: DRAFT · Reviewed by: ___

Step 6

Final project · 2. Mark each step

  1. For each step on your map, write Person, Copilot, Agent or Flow.
  2. Every approval and every decision about a person stays with a Person.
Stuck? Show a hint

Copilot: one-off drafting or summarizing. Agent: the same questions or drafts for many people. Flow: the same steps every time something happens.

Still stuck? Show another hint

Moving a form to an approver, logging a row, sending the same email: those are Flow steps. Approving is a Person step.
