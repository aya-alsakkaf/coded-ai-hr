How to use this
  1. Watch the trainer first. Then find the same card here.
  2. Download the file on the card. In Copilot Chat, start a new chat and attach it (the + or paperclip button).
  3. Click COPY, paste the request into Copilot, then press Enter.
  4. Check the answer against the file before you trust it: dates, numbers, names and rules.

Answers vary. Copilot may word things differently from the trainer's screen. That is normal, and it is why we check. Use only the fictional Mutla files here, or green tasks from your own work.

A timeline, not a verdict

Timeline and open questions
Put the events in the notes below in date order, in a table: date and time, what happened, who reported it. Then list the open questions. The notes are for an HR case file. HR has made no finding. Sunday 1 November, 07:30: The tool checklist at the South Mutla workshop is not signed (source: the shift log). Integration · One case

Task 2.6Copilot Chat + the appsYour turn · 45 min

One case, four outputs

  1. Open the case file for your unit track.
  2. Output 1: a Word summary of the case. Output 2: an Outlook message to the right person.
  3. Output 3: a brief for the meeting in the case. Output 4: an Excel summary or a PowerPoint briefing.
  4. Run the four-point review on each output: accuracy, confidentiality, neutrality, source alignment.
You hand in

Four reviewed outputs for your track's case: a Word summary, an Outlook message, a meeting brief, and an Excel summary or PowerPoint briefing.

Summary
You are an HR officer at Mutla. Summarize the attached case in one page: the facts, the dates, who owns what and the open questions. Use only the case file. Neutral and plain English.
Review
Review this draft against the attached case file. Check four things: accuracy of every date and number, any personal or confidential detail, neutral wording, and that every point comes from the file. List any problem with the line it is in.
Finished early? Extra task
  • Write your own review request for the riskiest output in your case, without copying the Review request on the prompt sheet. Add it to the Prompt Bank under Review.
What to look forStart with the summary; the other three outputs reuse it.
