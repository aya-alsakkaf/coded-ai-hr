How to use this
  1. Watch the trainer first. Then find the same card here.
  2. Download the file on the card. In Copilot Chat, start a new chat and attach it (the + or paperclip button).
  3. Click COPY, paste the request into Copilot, then press Enter.
  4. Check the answer against the file before you trust it: dates, numbers, names and rules.

Answers vary. Copilot may word things differently from the trainer's screen. That is normal, and it is why we check. Requests you write yourself are not on this sheet: write them with the checklist, and use the hints on the day's lab page if you are stuck. You are a Personnel officer at Mutla Oil Company. The note below is already redacted. It goes to the Personnel Supervisor. Task 2.1Your eyes + WordYour turn · 11 min

Redact a case note

  1. Open mutla-case-note-unredacted.docx in Word. Save a copy with -redacted at the end of the name.
  2. Find every detail that identifies a person or is sensitive.
  3. Replace names with roles (Employee A, Colleague B). Remove the civil ID, phone number, salary and medical reason.
  4. Read it once more. Could anyone outside HR tell who this is?

Agenda, brief and discussion points

Watch it in the slide. The trainer runs it with the room.
Prepare the meeting
You are a Personnel officer at Mutla Oil Company. We meet for 30 minutes on Monday to plan the orientation day for new joiners. Personnel, IT and L&D attend. Prepare an agenda, a one-paragraph background brief and three discussion points. The facts: 18 new joiners start on Sunday 6 December 2026. The orientation day runs from 8:00 to 13:00 in Training Room 2, head office. Personnel and Administration prepares the ID cards; IT prepares 18 laptop accounts. L&D runs the welcome session and the safety briefing. Open question: can the Medical Services check be done on the same day? Use only these facts. Give each agenda item an owner and minutes that add up to 30. Turn each open question into a discussion point. Short and practical. An agenda table (item, owner, minutes), then the brief, then the discussion points. Start with: DRAFT · Reviewed by: ___
What to look forDo the minutes add up to 30?
FilesNo file needed.
TakeawayAgenda, brief, discussion points: from approved facts only.

Block 6 · Map a workflow

Demo 2.10Copilot ChatWatch, then try

A story into a step table

Watch it in the slide. The trainer runs it with the room.
Map a story
Turn the process story below into a step table. The story: To book an external trainer, the Training Coordinator emails three providers for prices. She copies each price into a spreadsheet by hand. Her manager approves the choice by email, usually after three days. She then emails Procurement, who asks for the same three prices again. Nobody tells the trainer the room number until the day before. Do not improve the process. Only map it. Flag each step that has a delay, duplicated effort, unclear ownership or a repetitive step. Mark every step where a person must decide. A table: step, who, what happens, input, output, wait, problem, a person decides?
What to look forA step nobody owns is still a step.
FilesNo file needed.
TakeawayMap it as it is. Flag the problems. Do not fix them yet.
