Step 10

Task 2.8 · Build your form with Copilot in Forms

  1. Open forms.cloud.microsoft (or forms.office.com) and select New Form.
  2. In the Draft with Copilot box, write a request from your plan: the title, who answers, every question with its type and choices, which are required. Select Keep it.
  3. Check every question against your plan. Delete anything you did not ask for. Fix types and choices by hand.
  4. Settings: only people in your organization can respond. Preview it on your phone.
  5. Send the link to your partner. Answer each other's form twice, with made-up answers.
Stuck? Show a hint

Copilot follows a list best: give it every question, its type and its choices.

Still stuck? Show another hint

No Draft with Copilot? Ask Copilot Chat for your questions as a numbered list, each choice on its own line. Paste it into Word, save it, then use Quick import in Forms.

Still stuck? Show the prompt
