Safe-use card

Before you type anything into Copilot, sort the task. Your company's AI and data policy always comes first.

Green, amber, red Three risks Policy first

Green: go ahead

Fictional or public data, and your own drafting.

  • Practice on the Mutla files
  • Improve the tone of your own announcement
  • Explain a COUNTIF formula

Amber: approved account, minimum data

Internal HR information, only in your company's approved Copilot account, with names removed.

  • Summarize an approved policy
  • Count training requests by unit with employee codes, no names

Red: never in a prompt

Civil IDs, named salaries, medical details, case details that name people, personal files.

  • A case note with names
  • A salary list with names
  • A sick-leave certificate
Three risks, three habits
RiskWhat it looks likeThe habit
Wrong factsCopilot predicts words. It can be fluent and wrong.Check every date, number and rule against the source.
Data in the wrong placeA personal account, or a note with names and salaries.Sort first: green, amber or red. Redact before you type.
Decisions handed overA draft that sets a grade, blames a person or promises an exception.Copilot drafts. Authorized HR staff decide.
Your company's policy comes first
  • Ask your IT or information-security team which Copilot account your company approves for HR work.
  • If your company has no approved Copilot account, amber becomes red.
  • Grades, pay, ratings, discipline and selection stay with authorized HR staff, whatever the tool can do.
  • When you are not sure, treat the task as red and ask first.
