Take-home guide

Repeat what you built, on your own work, safely. Start with green tasks. Your company's AI and data policy comes first.

Your own work, safely First week, first month Policy first
01

Your first week

  1. Write one five-input request a day on a green task.
  2. Run the four-point review on each result before it goes anywhere.
  3. Read the license label under your name, so you know which Copilot you have.
02

Your first month

  1. Pick one task your unit does every month.
  2. Build a prompt set or an HR assistant for it, with approved files only.
  3. Ask a colleague from another unit to run it with no instructions, then fix what broke.
  4. Save the tested requests in the Prompt Gallery and share them with your team.
!The habit · every time. Copilot drafts. You check. Authorized HR staff decide. Your company's AI and data policy comes first.
