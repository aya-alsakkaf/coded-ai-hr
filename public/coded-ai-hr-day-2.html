Your turn

6 hands-on tasks. The timer runs on the main screen. Every prompt is on your prompt sheet.

Your turn · Task 2.1 · 15 min · in pairs

What changed between v3 and v4

  1. Attach mutla-allowances-policy-v3.docx and mutla-allowances-policy-v4.docx to a new chat.
  2. Run the compare request.
  3. Open both files. Check each change Copilot lists against the sections.
  4. Write a change table: section, version 3, version 4.
15:00 on the clock · Copilot Chat · Word
You hand in: A change table with section numbers.
Look for: The housing cap, the transport grades and one new section.
Finished early? Draft a three-line note to staff about the changes, every number unchanged.
mutla-allowances-policy-v3.docxmutla-allowances-policy-v4.docx
Your turn · Task 2.2 · 12 min · on your own

Announce and reply

  1. Use mutla-email-thread-attendance.docx and your decisions summary.
  2. Request 1: the staff announcement, in a warm and clear tone, every date and rule unchanged.
  3. Request 2: a reply to Mubarak's request in message 29.
  4. Check every time and date against the thread.
12:00 on the clock · Copilot Chat · Outlook
You hand in: A staff announcement and a reply to Mubarak, every date and rule unchanged.
Look for: Mubarak's request is message 29. The earliest start is in message 24.
Finished early? Write a version of the announcement for line managers: what changes for them.
mutla-email-thread-attendance.docx
Your turn · Task 2.3 · 12 min · on your own

Timeline, not verdict

  1. Attach mutla-investigation-notes.docx to a new chat. The notes use roles, not names.
  2. Run the timeline request.
  3. Read the result for any finding, blame or recommended action. Delete it.
  4. Check each date against the notes.
12:00 on the clock · Copilot Chat
You hand in: A timeline and an open-questions list, with no conclusion in it.
Look for: One line in the notes is an opinion. It must not become a finding.
Finished early? Write the agenda for the follow-up meeting from mutla-meeting-transcript-staffing.docx.
mutla-investigation-notes.docx
Your turn · Task 2.4 · 15 min · in pairs

Two figures, checked

  1. Attach mutla-hr-trackers-q3-2026.xlsx. Run the count request on the Training sheet.
  2. Compare with the Control totals sheet. Ask Copilot for the rows behind the difference.
  3. Then ask for total vacancies in the Manpower sheet. Compare with the Control totals sheet.
  4. Find why each figure is wrong. Check it in Excel yourself.
15:00 on the clock · Copilot Chat + Excel
You hand in: The two corrected figures and the reason for each.
Look for: One total is too high and one is too low.
Finished early? Ask for a chart of training requests by unit after removing the duplicates. Check that the axis starts at zero.
mutla-hr-trackers-q3-2026.xlsx
Your turn · Task 2.5 · 10 min · on your own

Five slides for the managers' meeting

  1. Attach mutla-policy-change-brief.docx. Run the five-slide request (or Copilot in PowerPoint).
  2. Check every time, date and number against the brief.
  3. Fix anything that changed.
10:00 on the clock · Copilot Chat · PowerPoint
You hand in: A five-slide deck or outline, each number checked against the brief.
Look for: Five facts: the date, the start window, core hours, permissions, the pilot.
Finished early? Ask for speaker notes in plain English, two sentences per slide.
mutla-policy-change-brief.docx
Your turn · Task 2.6 · 45 min · on your own

One case, four outputs

  1. Open the case file for your unit track.
  2. Output 1: a Word summary of the case. Output 2: an Outlook message to the right person.
  3. Output 3: a brief for the meeting in the case. Output 4: an Excel summary or a PowerPoint briefing.
  4. Run the four-point review on each output: accuracy, confidentiality, neutrality, source alignment.
45:00 on the clock · Copilot Chat + the apps
You hand in: Four reviewed outputs for your track's case: a Word summary, an Outlook message, a meeting brief, and an Excel summary or PowerPoint briefing.
Look for: Start with the summary; the other three outputs reuse it.
Finished early? Write your own review request for the riskiest output in your case, without copying the Review request on the prompt sheet. Add it to the Prompt Bank under Review.
