Meetings, Reporting and Mapping Your HR Workflow

Protecting employee data, meetings and follow-ups, Excel figures you can check, summaries and slides, and a map of one HR process from request to result.

Day 2 of 3 · Wednesday 18 Nov 09:00-15:00 In person · CODED, Alsharekh room, Frontend Campus
The day in one line · 09:00-15:00

Day brief

Day 2 starts with the most important habit: what may go into a prompt, and how to redact what may not. Then you prepare a meeting, recap it into actions and follow it up in Outlook. You check Excel figures against a control total, turn them into a management summary and five slides, and end the day with a map of one HR process: every step, every owner, every wait and every human decision.

You build today: A redacted case note, a meeting pack and recap, two checked figures in a summary and slides, and a map of one HR process.

What you will be able to do

  • Sort any HR task as green, amber or red, and redact a note before Copilot sees it.
  • Prepare a meeting, recap it into actions, owners and deadlines, and follow it up in Outlook.
  • Check every Excel figure against a control total, and every slide against its source.
  • Map an HR process from request to result, and mark the delays, duplicates, owners and human decisions.
