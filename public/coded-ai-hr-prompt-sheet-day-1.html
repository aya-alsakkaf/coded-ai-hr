How to use this
  1. Watch the trainer first. Then find the same card here.
  2. Download the file on the card. In Copilot Chat, start a new chat and attach it (the + or paperclip button).
  3. Click COPY, paste the request into Copilot, then press Enter.
  4. Check the answer against the file before you trust it: dates, numbers, names and rules.

Answers vary. Copilot may word things differently from the trainer's screen. That is normal, and it is why we check. Use only the fictional Mutla files here, or green tasks from your own work.

Redact, then ask

Summarize a redacted note
Turn the note below into a short, neutral case summary for the HR file: what happened, the request and the next step. The note is already redacted. It goes to the Personnel Supervisor. Employee A, a technician, was absent on two days in late October. [Medical detail removed.] Employee A asks to work at a different site for one month. Colleague B can cover the shift. Neutral, short, plain English. Use only the note above. Do not guess any removed detail.
What to look forIf the summary guesses a removed detail, add: do not guess anything removed.

Task 1.2Your eyes + WordYour turn · 12 min

Redact a case note

  1. Open mutla-case-note-unredacted.docx in Word. Save a copy with -redacted at the end of the name.
  2. Find every detail that identifies a person or is sensitive.
  3. Replace names with roles (Employee A, Colleague B). Remove the civil ID, phone number, salary and medical reason.
  4. Read it once more. Could anyone outside HR tell who this is?
You hand in

Only on the redacted copy
Read the attached note. List any detail that could still identify a person or is sensitive. Do not repeat the detail itself; give its paragraph number.
Finished early? Extra task
  • On the redacted copy only, ask Copilot whether any personal detail is left.
